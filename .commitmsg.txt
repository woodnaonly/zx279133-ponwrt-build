docs(9132): the bench was addressing the vendor firmware, and the RED drops every frame for a zero length

The static ARP entry the bench installed with "arp -s" landed on the WLAN
adapter, so the wired adapter kept its own dynamic entry, learned from the
vendor firmware. Every unicast frame went to the vendor firmware and the
trial kernel was never addressed. With the entry bound to the wired adapter
(New-NetNeighbor -InterfaceIndex), the frames reach the RED - and the RED
drops all of them for a zero length, which the board's own queue geometry is
the first candidate for.
